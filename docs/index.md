## About This Project
This project combines a traditional scientific literature review with an original survey to investigate social media's impact on adolescents in the modern world. It was initially inspired by a conversation between the co-authors about the book *Stolen Focus: Why You Can't Pay Attention* by Johann Hari. We believe that the digital age has brought about an incredible number of tools that can help us learn and accomplish more than ever before; however, it has also brought its potential share of distractions and newfound difficulties. 

Navigating the world of social media is particularly important in the adolescent age range as social media services become more and more ubiquitous. Anecdotally, we hear a lot of differing opinions about social media's impact on our social skills, mental health, and overall well-being. So, we set out to investigate: **What effects does social media have on us? What can we learn from existing studies, and what have we yet to discover?** And finally, once we got some information, we took matters into our own hands as best we could to address the question: **How does social media impact those immediately around us?** 

We hope this project will answer some of those questions for you, and perhaps help find strategies to improve your own experiences with social media use.

## Disclaimer

*This site is intended to be educational, but does not provide professional medical or mental health advice. If you feel social media is significantly affecting your well-being or you are generally struggling with mental health, please talk with a trusted adult or health professional.*
