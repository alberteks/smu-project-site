**Teens are spending nearly 5 hours daily on social media. Here are the mental health outcomes** (URL: [https://www.apa.org/monitor/2024/04/teen-social-use-mental-health](https://www.apa.org/monitor/2024/04/teen-social-use-mental-health))  
*(Published April 2024\)*

* **41% of teens with the highest social media use rate their overall mental health as poor or very poor**, compared with 23% of those with the lowest use.   
    * **10%** of highest use group expressed **suicidal intent or self-harm** in the past 12 months compared with 5% of the lowest use group  
    * **17%** of highest users expressed **poor body image** compared with 6% of the lowest users  
* **U.S. teens average 4.8 hours on seven popular social media** apps (including YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok, all of which accounted for 87% of their social media time)   
    * 37% of teens say they spend 5 or more hours a day, 14% spend 4 to less than 5 hours a day, 26% spend 2 to less than 4 hours a day, and 23% spend less than 2 hours a day on these three apps  
* **60% of teens with the highest social media use report low parental monitoring and weak parental relationships** (out of those who said had poor or very poor mental health), compared with 25% of the highest frequency users who report high parental monitoring and strong parental relationships  
    * 22% of the highest users with poor parental relationships and monitoring expressed thoughts of suicide or self-harm compared with 2% of high users with strong parental relationships and monitoring
