*(Last Updated: April 2024\)*

* Social media has made it possible to have access to the latest news at any given moment, in real-time, right at your fingertips  
    * Most teens report preferring to get their news from social media and/or YouTube than traditional news sources  
    * Teens who get news from social media & YouTube are also much more likely to consume news from influencers and content creators who may not be reliable sources  
* Consuming news on social media might increase teens’ likelihood of participating in civic events & volunteering within community  
* Research has also shown, on the other hand, that too much exposure to distressing news can have negative impacts on well-being  
    * Systematic review of research articles about COVID-19 news consumption & mental health found that higher consumption of COVID-19 news on social media was related to adverse mental health outcomes, including depression & anxiety in adolescents/young adults 10-24 years old  
    * Literature review about children and adolescents’ reactions to war media coverage found that exposure to war media produces a range of emotions, and those reactions are often dependent on context; children with direct ties to the conflict or a family member involved in the war may experience more sadness. Even those far from the conflict may still experience feelings of fear, worry, sadness, and anger when viewing war media coverage  
    * Besides news coverage of current events, adolescents may also be impacted by FOMO (fear of missing out) and wanting to stay updated w/ friends on social media  
* Some things to do to stay connected while protecting mental well-being:  
    * Limiting the types of quantity of content you see  
        * Some resources for adjusting settings:   
            * Managing content controls – TikTok ([https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/content-controls/](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/content-controls/))   
            * Navigating tragic events (which can include distressing news coverage & social media content) – TikTok ([https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/tragic-events-support/](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/tragic-events-support/))   
            * Adjusting sensitive content controls – Instagram ([https://help.instagram.com/251027992727268/?helpref=related\_articles](https://help.instagram.com/251027992727268/?helpref=related_articles))   
            * How to stop seeing suggested posts with certain words or phrases – Instagram ([https://help.instagram.com/682833393310241/?cms\_platform=iphone-app\&helpref=platform\_switcher](https://help.instagram.com/682833393310241/?cms_platform=iphone-app&helpref=platform_switcher))   
            * How to manage and adjust recommended content – YouTube ([https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6342839?hl=en\&hl=en\&co=GENIE.Platform%3DiOS\&oco=1](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6342839?hl=en&hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DiOS&oco=1))   
    * Go directly to credible sources  
        * Some resources for media literacy and identifying credible sources:  
            * Is This Legit: A Media Literacy Skills Recap ([https://www.poynter.org/tfcn/2023/is-this-legit-a-media-literacy-skills-recap/](https://www.poynter.org/tfcn/2023/is-this-legit-a-media-literacy-skills-recap/))   
            * Echo Chambers: How They’re Created and How to Avoid Them ([https://www.poynter.org/tfcn/2023/echo-chambers-how-theyre-created-and-how-to-avoid-them/](https://www.poynter.org/tfcn/2023/echo-chambers-how-theyre-created-and-how-to-avoid-them/))   
            * Tips for Detecting Doctored or Out of Context Videos ([https://www.poynter.org/fact-checking/2023/tips-for-detecting-doctored-or-out-of-context-videos/](https://www.poynter.org/fact-checking/2023/tips-for-detecting-doctored-or-out-of-context-videos/))   
    * Be intentional about when you check social media  
        * E.g., consider putting phone away an hour before bed or try to avoid checking social media for news updates when you won’t have time to process the information you see, set tech-free times (like homework time, time spent with friends, & family mealtimes), switch on “do not disturb” for notifications  
    * Communicate w/ peers and trusted adults about concerning content you see online
